## One look, one behaviour

Before: 3 of 29 signed-in routes used the design-system frame (DsPage). Two competing frames
(DetailBody 760 wide, PortalPage) and bare pages (Reports, Data browser, Merge, Welcome pack).

After: every signed-in page sits in the same frame — sand background, 1100 wide, 24 padding —
and opens with the same title card (detail pages keep their header card in that frame):
PortalPage is DsPage; DetailBody, Welcome pack, Reports, Data browser, Merge, Settings, People,
Pending approval, Projects, Structure, Outputs, Conferences moved (4fe68ce, e50bf2f, f59c2c2).
An admin opening a researcher now sees that researcher's own v1 pages. Settings links to Data
browser and Reports fixed. Sidebar navy #16213A; Inter with Segoe UI fallback (e4b0f03).

Kept on purpose: muted text #6A6862, not #888680 (#888680 fails WCAG AA on the page background).
